Freshly prepared, good natural food.

It's printed on the awning outside, and it's still the idea. Cafe Parisienne is a family-run café, owned by Kazim and his wife, cooking big breakfasts, paninis and pasta for Battersea from early morning.

Sit under the skylights and the old photographs, or take a chair on the pavement when the sun is out. Regulars say it feels like family, and first-timers tend to come back.
